## Handing a problem child back
After your children are converted and their checkers have run, do not re-convert the whole chapter because one child is still wrong: call `dispatch_fix` with one issue per problem (id, child, where, what, rule). It records the plan and runs each child's fix session and re-check right away; its receipt says what got fixed, and you only integrate the current child artifacts. Its receipt already gives each child's outcome, so do not repeat or paraphrase that receipt in your reply — just continue with integration. Children you do not list are untouched. `figures/` and `images/` are project-level resources supplied at assembly time: a child referencing them is NOT non-self-contained and must not be handed back. Only paths into `_subdivision/…` or `node/result/…` are broken includes.
